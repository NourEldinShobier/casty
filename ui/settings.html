Audio

System sound and where it plays.

Sound
Send system soundViewers can choose to hear this machine.
On a viewer, the sound button cycles PC, Here, Both. "Here" mutes this machine and plays there. The volume slider adjusts Casty's own level on the viewer, or this machine's volume when sound stays here.
